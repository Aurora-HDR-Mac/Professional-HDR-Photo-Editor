## What is Aurora HDR?

![Aurora HDR Editing](https://cdn.filtergrade.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/03030307/13901728_10210481657264689_180826152_o-1024x640.jpg)

Aurora HDR is a professional-grade application designed for photographers who demand the highest quality in high dynamic range imaging. It transforms multiple exposures into a single, perfectly balanced photograph with incredible detail in both highlights and shadows. Beyond simple HDR merging, it serves as a comprehensive photo editor with advanced tools for color grading, tonal adjustment, and creative effects.

The software stands out with its unique Quantum HDR Engine, which uses artificial intelligence to analyze and blend images with unprecedented accuracy, eliminating ghosting and alignment issues. Users benefit from a wide array of features including precision tone mapping, custom LUT support for cinematic color grades, and a non-destructive layer-based workflow for maximum flexibility.

Aurora HDR is built for performance and integrates seamlessly into any creative workflow. It supports all major camera RAW formats and offers one-click presets crafted by top photographers for instant transformations, as well as deep manual controls for fine-tuning every aspect of the image. Its user-friendly interface ensures that both hobbyists and professionals can achieve stunning results, from natural-looking enhancements to dramatic artistic interpretations. The application is continuously updated to support the latest camera models and macOS features, ensuring compatibility and top performance on modern hardware.
